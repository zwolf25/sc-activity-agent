Demo cleanup

A one-time job that clears old records the agent did not create, at all of your Subscriber's locations.

What it changes, side effects and what it skips
  • Open proposals and invoices are rejected.
  • Open, in-progress and on-hold work orders are cancelled.
  • Completed work orders waiting on a step are confirmed.
  • Approved, paid and closed records are not touched.
  • Side effect: ServiceChannel dates a cancelled work order's completion on the day you cancel it and sets its NTE to 0, so dashboards can show a spike. Confirming a work order with a compliance hold or no sub-status also moves its completion date to today.
  • Always skipped: work orders the agent created or picked up, records with the protect label, work scheduled in the future, and anything updated in the last 30 days.
